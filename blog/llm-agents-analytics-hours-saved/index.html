I gave 100 hours a month back to my team with LLM agents

Everyone wants AI to generate insight. In my experience, the bigger win is letting it generate time.

At Cox Media Group I helped design LLM-powered analytics agents that took over the most soul-draining part of the job: the recurring pull-clean-summarize-format cycle that ate 100+ hours a month across the team.

Start with the boring wins

We didn't begin with a moonshot. We began with the monthly business review — a report so predictable you could set a watch by it. If a task is repetitive, rule-based, and well-documented, it's a candidate. If it needs judgment and context, keep a human in the loop.

Automate the sentence you've written a hundred times. Keep the sentence only you can write.

Guardrails matter more than cleverness

An agent that's confidently wrong is worse than no agent. We built in source citations, value-range checks, and a human sign-off before anything reached leadership. Trust is the product; the automation is just the delivery mechanism.

The real payoff

The hours we recovered didn't go back into more reports. They went into the work that actually needs a human: asking better questions, pressure-testing assumptions, and telling the story the data can't tell on its own.
