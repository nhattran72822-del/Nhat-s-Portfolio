What is Marketing Mix Modeling?

Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM) is a regression technique that separates what actually drives sales. We fit two years of weekly Sales ($) against each channel's spend, the retail (promotional) budget, and real-world events — so we can read every factor's isolated effect while holding the others constant.

Sales = β₀ + β₁·Search + β₂·Social + β₃·Programmatic + β₄·OTT + β₅·Video + β₆·Retail Budget + β₇·War + ε

What it predicts: the dollars of sales each channel returns per $1k spent — and, via the p-value, whether we can trust that number or it's just noise. Toggle the war factor to watch an external shock reshape the model.
